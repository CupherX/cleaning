About Brother Paul Cleaning Kenya

Founded in 2020, Brother Paul Cleaning has grown from a small Nairobi operation to the premier cleaning service in Kenya. What sets us apart is our unwavering commitment to quality, attention to detail, and personalized service.

Our founder, Paul Mwangi, started this business with a simple philosophy: treat every home and business as if it were your own. This customer-first approach has earned us countless loyal clients across Kenya and a reputation for excellence.

We're not just cleaners - we're your partners in maintaining a healthy, beautiful environment. Whether you need a one-time deep clean in your Karen home or regular maintenance for your Westlands office, we tailor our services to your exact needs.
